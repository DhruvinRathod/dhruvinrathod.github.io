ABOUT ME

I started with backend engineering. The AI work came later.

I’m a software engineer who likes understanding how a system works end to end, not just the piece sitting in front of me.

I joined Accenture in 2021 and spent the next few years working mainly with Java and Spring Boot. The work was practical and sometimes messy in the way enterprise software usually is: APIs, batch jobs, data migration, SQL, debugging, production issues, testing and gradual modernization. I was promoted to Software Engineering Analyst in 2023 and took on more ownership across those systems.

A piece of work I still remember well was a multithreaded Spring Batch validation utility. It automated part of a migration-validation process that had been taking roughly nine hours of manual effort each cycle. I enjoyed that project because the value was very easy to see.

I moved to Germany for my M.Sc. in Computer Science in 2025. Since then, I’ve been going deeper into Python, PyTorch, multilingual LLMs and ML security while keeping my backend engineering skills active. I’m most interested in work where software engineering and AI meet a real operational problem rather than existing as separate tracks.

IEEE has also been a meaningful part of my journey. Volunteering, mentoring and speaking pushed me to get better at explaining technical ideas, working with different people and taking responsibility outside my day-to-day code.
