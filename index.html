ABOUT ME

Engineering first. Curiosity always.

I’m a software engineer and M.Sc. Computer Science student at Paderborn University.

I started professionally in enterprise backend development, where reliability, data correctness and maintainability mattered every day. That experience shaped how I approach new technical areas: understand the system, build carefully, measure the result, and leave the workflow easier to operate than before.

Today I’m extending that foundation into multilingual LLM engineering. I enjoy the point where research ideas become reproducible pipelines, measurable experiments and software that other people can actually use.

I think in terms of problems, data flows, failure modes and maintainability first. Frameworks, libraries and models are implementation choices; the engineering skill is knowing how to use them to build and evaluate a dependable system.

I also value collaboration beyond code. Through IEEE volunteering, mentoring and speaking, I’ve learned to explain technical ideas clearly and work with people from different backgrounds.
